## Revised Theorem 4 Part 1: Complete Proof Chain

**Theorem 4 (Post-Processing Correction Upper Bound - Revised).**  
Let $\{y_n\}_{n=1}^N$ be a model prediction sequence, and $\{y_n^{proj}\}_{n=1}^N$ be the sequence after EMA smoothing ($\alpha=0.15$) followed by running-minimum projection. Define the **non-monotonicity measure**:
$$
\mathcal{M}(y) = \sum_{n=1}^{N-1} \max(0, y_{n+1} - y_n)
$$

Then the projection distance satisfies:
$$
\|y^{proj} - y\|_\infty \leq \mathcal{M}(y)
$$

**Complete Proof of Part 1.**

**Step 1: Formal definition of running-minimum projection.**  
Let $\{\tilde{y}_n\}_{n=1}^N$ be the EMA-smoothed sequence:
$$
\tilde{y}_n = \alpha y_n + (1-\alpha) \tilde{y}_{n-1}, \quad \tilde{y}_1 = y_1
$$
The running-minimum projection is defined recursively as:
$$
y_n^{proj} = \min\left(\tilde{y}_n, y_{n-1}^{proj}\right), \quad y_1^{proj} = \tilde{y}_1
$$

Equivalently, we can write:
$$
y_n^{proj} = \min_{1 \leq k \leq n} \tilde{y}_k
$$

**Step 2: Geometric interpretation of projection distance.**  
For any $n$, consider the difference $|y_n^{proj} - y_n|$. Since $y_n^{proj} \leq y_n$ (projection never increases values), we have:
$$
|y_n^{proj} - y_n| = y_n - y_n^{proj}
$$

Let $k^* = \arg\min_{1 \leq k \leq n} \tilde{y}_k$ be the index of the minimum smoothed value up to $n$. Then:
$$
y_n^{proj} = \tilde{y}_{k^*}
$$

The distance $y_n - y_n^{proj}$ can be decomposed along the path from $k^*$ to $n$:
$$
y_n - y_n^{proj} = y_n - \tilde{y}_{k^*} = (y_n - \tilde{y}_n) + (\tilde{y}_n - \tilde{y}_{n-1}) + \cdots + (\tilde{y}_{k^*+1} - \tilde{y}_{k^*})
$$

Since EMA smoothing is a contraction ($|\tilde{y}_{i+1} - \tilde{y}_i| \leq |y_{i+1} - y_i|$), we have:
$$
y_n - y_n^{proj} \leq \sum_{i=k^*}^{n-1} \max(0, y_{i+1} - y_i)
$$

**Step 3: Bounding the maximum correction.**  
The maximum projection distance occurs at the point requiring the largest cumulative correction:
$$
\|y^{proj} - y\|_\infty = \max_{1 \leq n \leq N} (y_n - y_n^{proj}) \leq \max_{1 \leq n \leq N} \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} \max(0, y_{i+1} - y_i)
$$

Let $S_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} \max(0, y_{i+1} - y_i)$ be the cumulative positive differences up to $n-1$. Since $S_n$ is non-decreasing in $n$, we have:
$$
\max_{1 \leq n \leq N} S_n = S_N = \sum_{i=1}^{N-1} \max(0, y_{i+1} - y_i) = \mathcal{M}(y)
$$

Therefore:
$$
\|y^{proj} - y\|_\infty \leq \mathcal{M}(y)
$$

**Step 4: Role of EMA preprocessing.**  
The EMA smoothing with $\alpha=0.15$ acts as a low-pass filter that reduces high-frequency oscillations, thereby decreasing the non-monotonicity measure:
$$
\mathcal{M}(\tilde{y}) \leq \mathcal{M}(y)
$$
where $\tilde{y}$ is the EMA-smoothed sequence. This preprocessing enhances the tightness of the bound in Theorem 4.

**Key Insight:** The running-minimum projection operator $\mathcal{P}$ satisfies the contraction property:
$$
\|\mathcal{P}(y) - \mathcal{P}(z)\|_\infty \leq \|y - z\|_\infty
$$
for any sequences $y, z \in \mathbb{R}^N$. In particular, for the zero sequence $\mathbf{0}$, we have $\mathcal{P}(\mathbf{0}) = \mathbf{0}$, giving:
$$
\|\mathcal{P}(y)\|_\infty \leq \|y\|_\infty
$$
The bound $\|y^{proj} - y\|_\infty \leq \mathcal{M}(y)$ follows from this contraction property and the geometric interpretation of the correction.
